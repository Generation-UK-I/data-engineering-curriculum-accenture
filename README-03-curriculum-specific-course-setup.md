# Academy course setup

This guide will help you prepare non-session things for the Academy delivery.

## Running Breakouts - Guidance

Slides for this are also in the [Instructor Onboarding training](https://ts.accenture.com/:f:/r/sites/AccentureSoTFY24/Shared%20Documents/General/Instructor%20Onboarding%20Training%20Material?csf=1&web=1&e=KOYNFW) in the [Instructor Tools session](https://ts.accenture.com/:f:/r/sites/AccentureSoTFY24/Shared%20Documents/General/Instructor%20Onboarding%20Training%20Material/Instructor%20Tools?csf=1&web=1&e=5h3ncd):

Via some feedback over a few academies a few useful bits of feedback have come up:

- Breakouts of 2-3 allow folks to communicate well without there being too many voices all at once
- Breakouts of 4+ tend to end up with someone not feeling they can contribute, or can't have their voice heard
- If possible it is good to place less confident people with at least one who is more confident in the subject matter
- Remind them to read through the exercise together before starting
- Suggest to them they have a nominated driver and co-pilots, so they know who is doing what. It helps if anyone less confident is the driver.
- Suggest they work on a shared set of code and/or use a Live Share (if there is time to set it up) so they all have one version of the truth to talk about
- Suggest for longer breakouts (say > 10 mins or i.e. 15-30 mins+) they rotate the driver half way through - though this only works well if there is a Live Share already working.

### Suggestions to give the cohort:

- When you join breakouts, please start with ‘how does everybody feel’ to work out who is confident and who is less confident amongst yourselves
- You can ask for help either using the ‘Ask for help’ button (in Google Meet) or (if no-one is in the main call) - tagging instructors on slack/teams/etc
- All of you need to take responsibility for managing the group, including telling people/each other if they’re talking too much or dominating the conversation
- Encourage people who are quieter to contribute more - give less confident people room to speak and give them time
