## Hackathons

We found hackathons to be another good way to get people doing some coding in a more relaxed and social environment. Hackathons work well in both the first and second part of the course:

- In the first part of the course, while learners are still doing their mini-projects, hackathons are a good way for them to start working on code together, and getting more used to things like pairing and mobbing
- In the second part of the course, where learners were divided out into their group projects, we made sure to do hackathons which 'mixed people up' so they were working with people who weren't in their project team. This was a good way to keep people in touch with people who weren't in their project groups.

Generally aimed for about 2 hours or so, not expecting the teams to complete the entire challenge in that time. We just set up the teams, gave them the brief, and let them off to make an attempt at it.

Please refer to the pdf files in this directory for the text of the hackathons we ran.
