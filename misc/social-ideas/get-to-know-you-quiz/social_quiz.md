## KNOW YOUR COLLEAGUES QUIZ

### Please answer the below questions and return the answers to us. We will use a selection of the answers to create a quiz so everyone can find out a few facts about each other.

### Don't feel like you have to answer EVERY question, but please do try to answer a few. Also obviously don't write anything that you would not be comfortable for everyone else to know.

- What is your favourite food/dish?
- What was your favourite ever holiday?
- Have you ever lived abroad? If so, where?
- What is your favourite genre of music?
- What is your favourite film?
- What are your hobbies?
- If you could live anywhere, where would you live?
- Do you have any phobias?
- What's the worst gift you have ever received?
- Do you have any pet peeves or things that any you?
- Do you (or did you growing up) play any sports?
- Who is your hero?
- Do you have any 'claim to fame'?
- What's an interesting fact about you?
- What is a song you know all the words to?
- What job would you be terrible at and why?
- What's your favourite book?
- What other languages can you speak? (Python not included...)
- Do you play any musical instruments?
- If you could have one superpower, what would it be?
