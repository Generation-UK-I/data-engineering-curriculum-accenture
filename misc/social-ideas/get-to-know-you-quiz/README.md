## Get to know you quiz

We did this in the first few weeks of the course, and it was a really fun way of breaking the ice and getting people to know some fun things about each other.

The `social_quiz.md` file was distributed to all the learners for them to fill in and return to one of the instructors. The instructor then used the answers to develop a set of quiz questions based on everyone's answers.

The whole group gathered on a call then and the instructor 'hosted' the quiz, asking the questions to the group, who would each write down their answers.

At the end, everyone sent their answer sheet back to the instructor for marking over the following day or two. The host also ran through the answers to the questions (which is the most fun part) on the call, and gently probed each member of the group when they were the answer to a question, to give a bit more context.

It was great to get everyone out of their shell and share something about themselves.

The only key thing to keep in mind when making the quiz from everyone's response, is to try make sure everyone is represented as an answer to at least one of the questions
