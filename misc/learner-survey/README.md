## Learner Survey

This is something we first developed for Sainsburys, to assist them in considering what may be the right team to place each of the learners in after the bootcamp had wrapped up.

However, it may be a useful self-reflection tool for any group of learners to think about the parts of the course they have most enjoyed or been most suited to, which will help them identify potentially suitable roles in future. Some of the questions reference Sainsbury's but they can be easily amended to remove these references.
