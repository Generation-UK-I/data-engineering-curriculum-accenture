## Collections of resources which made for good socials when remote

### Codenames

https://codenames.game/

- Works well for a group anywhere from 4 - 12 or so
- Basic idea is the group is split in two teams - and each team has one 'spymaster' who must try give clues to their own team to guide them to their words on a shared grid of words, while avoiding the other team's words
- Proved a very popular game and very easy to do remote

---

### Psyche

This is an app, so download from Google Play or Apple App Store

- We tried this once and it was quite popular
- Everyone given the same question and you have to try make up a fake answer to it that is still believable. Once everyone has done this, you vote for the answer you think is correct. So you are trying to find the actual answer versus everyone's made up answers. You get points for getting the actual correct answer, or if someone else votes for your fake answer

### skribbl.io

https://skribbl.io/

- Simple but fun Pictionary style game where it rotates through each member of the group to draw something and everyone else has to guess what it is.
- You get points for guessing someone else's drawing quickly, and also for how quickly the group guesses your drawing.
