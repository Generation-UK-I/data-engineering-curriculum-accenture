## The problem solving mantra!

If I encounter a problem I will:
    Spend 15 minutes looking for an answer
If I cannot find an answer I will:
    Ask a friend to help me
If I ask a friend to help me, we will:
    Spend 15 minutes looking for an answer
If we cannot find an answer I will:
    Ask the team to help me
If I ask the team to help me, we will:
    Spend 15 minutes looking for an answer
If the team cannot find an answer I will:
    Step away from the problem and come back to it later when I am fresh and ready
When I am fresh and ready I will:
    Be in a better place to solve that problem.
